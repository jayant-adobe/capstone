name
Profile Cards
description

Dynamic team/contributor grid driven by a data sheet. Since the About Us people aren't backed by pages, the block reads a published data sheet (/us/en/contributors.json), filters to a team (contributor or guide), sorts by order and renders circular-portrait profile cards (name, role, social links). Row team is required; optional limit caps the count. Used for the two About Us people grids.
