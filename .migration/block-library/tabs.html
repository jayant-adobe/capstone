Overview

Climb some of New Zealand's most spectacular rock with professional guides. With countless routes to choose from, this trip suits confident beginners through to seasoned climbers.

Itinerary

Day 1 — Foundational climbing techniques on the crags near Fauchere.

Day 2 — A committing 60-metre waterfall climb with a big reward at the top.

What to Bring

All technical equipment is provided. Bring weather-appropriate clothing, sturdy footwear and your own provisions for the day.
